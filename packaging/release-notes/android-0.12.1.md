StrataOS Android 0.12.1: the app now has its own releases. An app installed at 0.12.0 finds this update by itself; afterwards it follows the `android-v…` releases.

- Recycle bin: deleted files are restored at their original place.
- Updates: the app looks for its own releases even when NAS and desktop releases are published in between.
- Notifications: a "Mark as read" button marks the NAS notification read (also in the bell of the web interface) without opening the app.
- Widget: processor and memory use under the storage.

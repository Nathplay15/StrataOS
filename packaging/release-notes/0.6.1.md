StrataOS 0.6.1: Raspberry Pi 5 support, a much better file viewer, grouped updates from the Multi-NAS Console, and fixes.

## Installation

- **PC / server**: `amd64` ISO (BIOS and UEFI). **UEFI ARM board**: `arm64` ISO.
- **Raspberry Pi 3 / 4 / 400 / CM4 / 5**: one image, `strataos-0.6.1-raspberrypi.img.xz`, for every model. Write it to a microSD card or a USB SSD and power the Pi on.
- **Existing Debian 13 system**: `sudo apt install ./strataos_0.6.1_<arch>.deb`.
- **Already on 0.6.0**: **Updates → Update StrataOS** in the web interface.

## What's new

**Files opens (almost) everything**
- Fixed: PDFs showed "authentication required" instead of the document. They now open in a built-in viewer (pages, zoom, search, print).
- Word and Excel files (and CSV, ODS) preview in the browser, without OnlyOffice.
- PowerPoint and other documents preview through the optional "Document previews" component (LibreOffice, installed from Settings → Components).
- Any video plays: files the browser can't read (MKV, AVI, H.265…) are repackaged or converted on the fly, with the Intel graphics chip when there is one. Subtitles and audio tracks can be chosen.
- Every audio format, HEIC/TIFF/RAW photos, and archives (zip, tar, 7z, RAR) with extraction.
- An "Open with" menu, a details panel (with SHA-256), keyboard navigation between files, full screen.

**Raspberry Pi**
- Raspberry Pi 5 support, with the Raspberry Pi kernels (Debian's don't support the Pi 5 yet). The rest of the system stays Debian.
- The image is four times smaller (265 MB instead of 1 GB). Docker and the UPS tools are installed on demand.

**Multi-NAS Console**
- Grouped updates: update several NAS at once or one after the other, with live progress. Each NAS must allow it first, and the two NAS of a high-availability pair are never updated at the same time.

**Media and surveillance**
- DLNA and music settings follow renamed or deleted shared folders. Shared folders can now be renamed.
- Casting survives a restart of the NAS.
- H.265 cameras play in the browser; Frigate recordings are listed in Surveillance.

**WireGuard**
- IPv6 through the outgoing tunnel, and IPv6 blocked when the tunnel has none (no leak).
- The kill switch also works in "whole NAS" mode, without cutting the local network or the WireGuard server.
- A warning before a device expires.

**App Center and translations**
- App descriptions and settings are translated into the 8 interface languages.
- Fixed: many Spanish texts of 0.6.0 were shown in German.

## Upgrading from 0.6.0

Update from the interface: everything is kept. Raspberry Pi systems installed from the 0.6.0 image keep their Debian kernel; the Raspberry Pi kernels (and Pi 5 support) come with the new image. On a Raspberry Pi with the Raspberry Pi kernels, iSCSI targets aren't available (the kernel lacks LIO).

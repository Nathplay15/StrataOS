StrataOS Android 0.12.2: a new look for the app. It works with StrataOS 0.12 NAS; installed apps find this update by themselves.

- **Home**: a blue header with search, transfers, notifications and your NAS at a glance, then a grid of colourful shortcuts (Files, Photos, Status, Storage, Backup, Folder sync, Applications, Assistant, virtual machines, VPN, users) and cards for photo backup, photos, storage and apps.
- **Navigation**: four tabs, Home, Files, Photos and **Me**. Me gathers your account, photo backup, folder sync, share links, the NAS status and management, the app settings, updates and sign-out. Manage and the Assistant open from Home.
- **Files**: a grid by default, with large icons by file type (documents, PDF, spreadsheets, presentations, archives…).
- **Photos**: an edge-to-edge grid.
- **Status and storage**: clearer cards, with health labels and usage bars.
- **First launch**: a short tour of the app, and new connection screens.
- Fixed: a virtual machine's USB list now shows which VM a device is given to.

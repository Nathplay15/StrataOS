StrataOS 0.15.0: a real media center — the cinema library of the NAS matched with TMDB, a full-screen menu on the TV over HDMI, subtitles fetched or generated — plus video surveillance with event clips and timelines, and a security journal that alerts on sign-ins from new devices.

## Installation

- **Already on 0.14.0**: **Updates → Update StrataOS** in the web interface.
- New installs: `amd64` / `arm64` ISO, `strataos-0.15.0-raspberrypi.img.xz`, or `sudo apt install ./strataos_0.15.0_<arch>.deb`. The desktop and Android apps have their own releases.

## New

**Cinema and media**
- The films and series of the shares get a real media center: the library fetches posters, synopses, actors and ratings from TMDB by itself; continue watching resumes exactly where you stopped, per account, on every screen; auto-collections (added this week, top rated, franchises), watch-together lists shared between accounts, family profiles hiding content above a rating cap.
- 10-foot interface: large cards with hover zoom, the trailer plays on poster hover, a screen saver with a slow wall of posters and the clock, and keyboard/gamepad navigation (A = ok, B = back, L/R = shelves).
- Subtitles are found automatically (OpenSubtitles, by IMDb id or file hash, best match in your preferred languages, saved next to the video) or generated from the audio by a local speech-to-text model (whisper.cpp); `.srt`/`.vtt` sidecars are picked up by the player, with adjustable size, color and outline, and a night mode that limits brightness and volume in the evening.
- **HDMI out**: plugged to a TV, the NAS shows the full-screen cinema menu on it (a kiosk browser on the detected display), driven with a keyboard, a gamepad, or the PWA remote control (Settings → Console screen).

**Video surveillance**
- Motion events on a per-camera day timeline, live view (HLS), and a clip cut around each event (a few seconds before and after, automatically pruned), carried by the push notification.
- Detection zones drawn on the picture (movement outside them is ignored), and an optional local classifier that labels events (person, vehicle…) and keeps shadows and foliage out of the notifications. Frigate stays available as an engine with its own object detection.

**Assistant**
- Sign in with a Mistral account (OAuth device flow): the NAS runs the sign-in, refreshes the token itself, and stores no long-lived key.
- Answers about your documents cite the files they read, with clickable references.
- Scheduled AI reports ("every Monday, summarize the NAS health, the new photos and the failed backups") sent as a notification or email, and scheduled actions in plain language ("stop the test VM every evening at 11 pm") created as routines and confirmed in the interface.

**Files, photos and migration**
- File versions beside each modified file (snapshot history, restorable), and the ZFS snapshots of a share browsable read-only in the file browser ("this folder yesterday at 4 am") with a restore in two clicks.
- Photos: on-this-day memories and automatic monthly recaps in the gallery, a duplicate review screen, and a light photo editor (rotate, crop, light) without leaving the NAS.
- Migration assistant: import from another NAS (SMB, NFS or rsync daemon) into a share, on demand or on a schedule.
- Built-in text editor for Markdown (with preview) and code files.

**Mobile and PWA**
- Web push notifications on the lock screen: camera events, disk alerts, finished backups, assistant reports.
- Automatic photo upload in the background (periodic sync, persistent folder handles, a pause and a rule per folder).
- The Android app syncs folder pairs (a phone folder and a NAS folder kept alike, deletions waiting 30 days in the pair's `.trash`).

**Sign-in and security**
- Security journal: every sign-in per user, an alert on a sign-in from a new device, one page to review everything. Passkeys and two-factor sign-in, WireGuard server with a QR code to scan and integrated dynamic DNS.
- Audit fixes: the subtitle language from OpenSubtitles is sanitized before it becomes a file name (path traversal closed), webhook/ntfy sends are guarded against reaching the NAS itself or cloud metadata (SSRF), the app proxy verifies TLS certificates off-loopback, and `npm audit` now runs in CI.

**Apps**
- One-click updates of the installed Docker apps, with automatic rollback to the previous image when the app does not come back up, and a health alert (with recent logs) for containers that crash repeatedly.

**Supervision and energy**
- A generated monthly/weekly letter: disk usage over the period, new photos and albums, failed backups, apps updated.
- User quotas visible on each account's page, with an alert before the quota is reached.
- The fleet dashboard also shows the NAS devices outside the cluster (simple read-only checks).
- Night energy plan: disks spin down on a schedule and the consumption is followed when the hardware reports it.

**Accounts, calendars**
- CalDAV and CardDAV: the calendars and address books of the accounts sync natively with iPhone, Android and Thunderbird.

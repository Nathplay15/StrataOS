StrataOS Android 0.13.1: fixes. It works with StrataOS 0.15 NAS; installed apps find this update by themselves.

- **Lock screen**: unlocking the app (fingerprint, face or PIN) returns to the screen you were on (a film, a folder) instead of Home.
- **Back where you left**: when Android closes the app in the background to free memory, it reopens on the same tab, page and section, and a film or video comes back at the same position, paused.
- **Films and videos**: playback pauses when the screen locks, in full screen or in picture-in-picture, and goes on from the same position once you unlock.
- **Messages of the NAS in your language**: error messages, reasons and task titles sent by the NAS are now in the language of the app (with StrataOS 0.15.4 on the NAS).
- **Administrator accounts**: from the app, the password, role, second factor and keys of an administrator account can no longer be changed, and no new administrator can be created (a lost phone must not give access to another administrator's account); use a browser for these.
